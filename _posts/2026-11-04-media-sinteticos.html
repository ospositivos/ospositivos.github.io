Despropositamos peça para fotografia do crescente fosso entre tv-e-o-que-vês:

For a time, the photograph or moving image acquired its own evidential authority: seeing something recorded was, with reasonable caveats, grounds for accepting that an event had occurred. Photography allowed us to see somewhere we had not been, film extended that principle, television took it further and the internet collapsed geography altogether. Artificial intelligence unsettles that relationship.

Convincing images can now depict events that never happened. This creates a curious reversal. The more sophisticated our technologies of representation become, the less the image alone can tell us about where it came from. This changes the nature of authenticity.
